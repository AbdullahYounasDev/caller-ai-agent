# ClinicHMS — Frequently Asked Questions

## How many practitioners can use ClinicHMS?

The Starter plan supports up to 2 practitioners, Growth supports up to 10, and Enterprise supports an unlimited number of practitioners across multiple branches.

## Appointment Reminders

See below for details.

ClinicHMS automatically sends SMS and email reminders to patients 24 hours and again 2 hours before each scheduled appointment. Reminder templates can be customized with the clinic's own name and tone of voice from the settings page.

## Can I import patients from another system?

Yes. During onboarding, the Nyron team imports your existing patient roster from a spreadsheet or an export from your previous system, mapping names, dates of birth, contact details and insurance information into ClinicHMS.

## Does ClinicHMS integrate with pharmacies?

The pharmacy module, included from the Growth plan upward, lets a clinic manage prescriptions and stock levels for an in-house pharmacy. It is not included on the Starter plan.

## What happens to my data if I cancel?

Exported data remains downloadable for 90 days after cancellation, after which it is permanently deleted. See the Returns and Refund Policy document for full details.
