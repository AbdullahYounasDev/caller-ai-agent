Nonna's Sunday Ragu

Ingredients

500 grams of ground beef, 200 grams of ground pork, one onion, two carrots, two celery stalks, a can of San Marzano tomatoes, a glass of red wine, olive oil, salt, pepper and a bay leaf.

Method

Chop the onion, carrots and celery finely and cook them slowly in olive oil until soft. Add the beef and pork and brown well. Pour in the red wine and let it reduce completely. Add the tomatoes and bay leaf, season with salt and pepper, and simmer on low heat for at least three hours, stirring occasionally and adding a little water if it gets too dry. Serve over tagliatelle with grated Parmesan.
