# Availability and Support Hours

## Regions Served

Nyron Software Solutions currently serves customers in Italy, the European Union and the United Kingdom. Our infrastructure runs from data centers in Frankfurt (EU primary), Milan (regional edge) and London (UK).

## Support Hours by Plan

### Starter and Lite plans
Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 CET, email support only.

### Growth and Pro plans
Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 CET, email and phone support.

### Enterprise plan
24 hours a day, 7 days a week, phone, email and a dedicated Slack channel.

## Uptime Commitment

| Plan tier | Uptime SLA |
|---|---|
| Starter / Lite | 99.5% |
| Growth / Pro | 99.9% |
| Enterprise | 99.9% with service credits |

If monthly uptime falls below the committed SLA, affected customers receive service credits according to the schedule in their contract.

## Holiday Closures

Support is closed on Italian public holidays. On UK and EU bank holidays, a skeleton support crew remains available for Enterprise customers only.
