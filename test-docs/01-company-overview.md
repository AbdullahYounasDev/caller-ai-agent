# Nyron Software Solutions

## About Us

Nyron Software Solutions S.r.l. is a business software company founded in 2019 and headquartered in Milan, Italy. We build vertical SaaS products for small and mid-sized businesses across Italy, the wider European Union and the United Kingdom.

## Our Products

**ClinicHMS** is a hospital and clinic management system covering patient records, appointment scheduling, billing and pharmacy workflows for clinics and small hospitals.

**StorePulse** is a retail and inventory management platform covering point-of-sale integration, multi-branch stock synchronization and sales analytics for shops and small supermarket chains.

**Nyron Connect** is an announced integration hub that will link ClinicHMS and StorePulse to third-party accounting and CRM tools. It has not been released yet, and there is no pricing or availability information for it today.
